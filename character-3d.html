Little cube. Big personality.

A genuinely three-dimensional little hero. His face lives on the front of the cube; when he runs, he turns his whole body to the side. When he jumps, he performs a forward head-over-heels somersault in the direction of travel, then can land upside down and slide.

Signature cube stunt
